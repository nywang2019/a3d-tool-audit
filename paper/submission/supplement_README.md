# Supplementary material guide

This archive supports the Applied Intelligence manuscript:

**The Wrong Tool Looks Right: Auditing Executable Goal-Irrelevant Tool Use in
Multi-Step LLM Agents**

**A3D:** Affordance-Aware Audit and Diagnosis.

- **Journal:** Applied Intelligence
- **Authors:** Nianyi Wang, Tiejun Wang, Weilan Wang
- **Affiliations:** School of Mathematics and Computer Science; Key Laboratory
  of Linguistic and Cultural Computing of Ministry of Education; Gansu
  Provincial Engineering Research Center of Multi-modal Artificial
  Intelligence, Northwest Minzu University, Lanzhou, China
- **Corresponding author:** Nianyi Wang, livingsailor@gmail.com
- **Supplementary item:** Online Resource 1

Contents:

- `diagnostic_protocol.md`: rule order and trace-metric definitions;
- `dataset_card.md`: data provenance, intended use, and release status;
- `derived_analysis.md`: generated main-effect and valid-trace summary;
- `gating_error_transitions.md`: descriptive intervention transitions;
- `case_studies/`: four generated A3D reports from saved trials;
- `tables/`: machine-readable CSV and readable Markdown versions of the paper
  tables;
- `reproducibility.md`: result directories, model configuration, and rebuild
  procedure;
The error categories are deterministic rule-derived diagnostics. They are not
human-validated ground truth.
